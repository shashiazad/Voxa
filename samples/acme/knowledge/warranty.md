# Warranty and Repairs

Every Acme Electronics product includes a one year limited warranty that covers manufacturing defects. Accidental damage, water damage, and normal wear are not covered. The Acme Care plan extends coverage to three years and adds one accidental damage claim per year; it costs 9 dollars per month for laptops and 4 dollars per month for headphones.

To make a warranty claim, the customer needs the order number or serial number. Support will create a ticket and send a prepaid shipping label by email. Repairs usually take 7 to 10 business days from the day the device arrives at our repair center. If a repair is not possible, we replace the product with an equivalent model.

Battery capacity that drops below 80 percent within the first year is treated as a defect and is covered by the warranty.
