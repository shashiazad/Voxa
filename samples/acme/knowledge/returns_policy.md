# Acme Electronics Return Policy

Acme Electronics accepts returns within 30 days of delivery for most products. Items must be in their original packaging with all accessories included. To start a return, the customer needs the order number and the email address used at checkout.

Opened headphones and earbuds can be returned within 30 days, but only if they are in working condition and the ear tips are included, for hygiene reasons. Laptops and tablets can be returned within 14 days of delivery and must be factory reset before they are sent back.

Refunds are issued to the original payment method within 5 to 7 business days after the returned item arrives at our warehouse. Return shipping is free for defective items. For change-of-mind returns, a flat shipping fee of 7 dollars is deducted from the refund.

Items that are not eligible for return include gift cards, downloadable software, and products marked as final sale. Custom-configured computers can only be returned if they arrive defective.
