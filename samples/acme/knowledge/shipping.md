# Shipping and Delivery

Standard shipping is free on orders over 50 dollars and takes 3 to 5 business days within the continental United States. Expedited shipping takes 2 business days and costs 12 dollars. Overnight shipping is available for 25 dollars when the order is placed before 2 PM Eastern time.

Orders are packed and handed to the carrier within 1 business day. A tracking number is sent by email as soon as the order ships. Customers can also ask our support line for the current status of an order using the order number.

Acme Electronics ships to the United States and Canada. International shipping outside of North America is not available at this time. Orders cannot be shipped to PO boxes when expedited or overnight shipping is selected.

If a package is marked as delivered but the customer cannot find it, they should check with neighbors and wait 24 hours, then contact support so we can open an investigation with the carrier.
